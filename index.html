Earth Movement ×

GNSS Movement Vectors

This tab will show GNSS station movement vectors and vertical uplift/subsidence trends across Iceland.

Map legend
▲ Uplift / upward vertical trend
▲ Subsidence / downward vertical trend
→ Horizontal movement direction

Status: GNSS stations are shown as coloured triangle markers using the 90-day movement trend.

Red ▲ indicates uplift/upward vertical trend above +10 mm/yr.

Blue ▲ indicates subsidence/downward vertical trend below -10 mm/yr.

Grey ▲ indicates stable vertical trend within ±10 mm/yr, or N/A where no current movement rate is available.

Gold arrows show general horizontal direction from the 90-day N/E trend only, not overall movement distance.

Movement values are shown as 90-day trend rates in mm/yr, based on the latest available IMO GNSS update. Values may be affected by weather, station conditions, and data processing.
